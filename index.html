What is GCK Lab?+
GCK Lab is a web tools studio based in Karnataka, India. We build practical, focused tools for Indian freelancers, job seekers, and small business owners. Current live tools: IncomeWave (income & tax tracker) and ResumeWave (ATS resume builder for the Indian job market).
Are the tools free to use?+
Yes — core features of all GCK Lab tools are completely free and always will be. Some tools offer optional paid upgrades for power users. No credit card is needed to start using any tool.
What is IncomeWave?+
IncomeWave is a free freelancer finance dashboard at incomewave.gcklab.com. It helps you track irregular income, estimate advance tax, manage multi-currency payments, and see your cash flow clearly — built specifically for Indian freelancers and gig workers.
What is ResumeWave?+
ResumeWave is a free ATS resume builder at resumewave.gcklab.com. It creates Naukri RChilli-safe resumes with the right Indian date formats, keywords for top MNCs, and a dedicated fresher mode for campus placement applicants. It also includes a LinkedIn optimizer and ATS score checker.
When will the upcoming tools launch?+
Invoice Chaser and Bio & Profile Writer are actively in development. GST Explainer, WhatsApp Reply Generator, Legal Notice Writer, YouTube Script Writer, and Caption Writer are all planned for 2026. Follow us or email us to be notified when tools go live.
How do I contact GCK Lab?+
Email us at gurucharan.k@zohomail.in or reach us on WhatsApp at +91 82963 72184. We respond to every message personally within 24–48 hours on business days.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. We take data security seriously. All tools use HTTPS encryption. We do not store your resume text, financial data, or any personal input after your session ends. We never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
